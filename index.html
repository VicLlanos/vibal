04 / DESIGN METHOD

From first reference
to clear deliverable.

A disciplined workflow keeps the design understandable, reviewable, and ready for the next person in the production chain.

01

Understand

Review the brief, reference dimensions, function, constraints, and intended use.

02

Build

Develop the primary geometry, component breakdown, and initial assembly structure.

03

Validate

Check clearances, interfaces, proportions, and drawing consistency; refine as needed.

04

Communicate

Prepare clean views, detailed drawings, templates, and organized hand-off material.
